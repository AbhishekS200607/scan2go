THE SCAN2GO PROCESS

How Smart Self-Checkout Works

Three simple steps from supermarket shelf to turnstile exit clearance.

1. Scan Product Barcode

Point your phone camera at any item barcode (EAN-13 / UPC). The server verifies price and stock instantaneously.

2. Instant Mobile Payment

Review cart subtotal with 5% GST tax. Complete digital payment in seconds to generate your single-use QR exit pass.

3. Turnstile QR Clearance

Present your single-use digital QR exit code at the store exit turnstile. Security staff verify items and disengage gate locks.
